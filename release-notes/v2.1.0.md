Skyblock Macro 2.1.0 for Minecraft 26.2 (Fabric). Put the jar in `.minecraft/mods` with Fabric Loader and Fabric API for 26.2.

> Hypixel's rules forbid macros, so accounts that use them can be banned. Use this at your own risk.

## New macros
- **Combat**: kills mobs by name tag around a saved spot, with a melee or right-click weapon (`/sm combat spot`).
- **Glacite Commissions**\*: mines or fights for the open commission and claims finished ones through a Royal Pigeon. The commission-to-blocks rules are editable.
- **Fossil Excavator**\*: puts Suspicious Scrap in, starts the excavator and uncovers tiles until the scrap runs out.

## Farming
- Pest action **Kill with vacuum**: clears pests near the farm, then rewarps.
- **Recorded movement** farm type: record a walk with `/sm echo record` / `/sm echo stop` and the macro replays it.
- **Garden visitors**\*: at each rewarp, walks to the visitors and accepts offers you already have the items for.

## Foraging, combat and mining
- **Tree routes**: `/sm forage add` saves trees; the foraging macro walks between them and chops instead of planting.
- **Slayer auto-start**\*: starts the next slayer quest when one ends.
- **Route point options**: `/sm route walk <n> <true|false>` and `/sm route wait <n> <ms>`.

## Everywhere
- **Auto sell**: sells listed items through `/trades` when the inventory fills (never the hotbar).
- **Active hours**: only runs inside a daily window such as `08:00-23:00`.
- **Panic key**: a keybind that only ever stops the macro.
- **Profit estimate** in the loot tracker, at Bazaar instant-sell prices.
- **Menu solvers**: Ultrasequencer, Chronomatron, Melody's Harp, and Forge auto-claim\*.

\* Not yet tested in game. These features click through SkyBlock menus by item name; every name they look for is a setting, so a wrong
guess can be fixed in the GUI. When something isn't found they stop or skip and say what was missing.
